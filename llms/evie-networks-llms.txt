# Evie Networks

> Australian electric-vehicle charging infrastructure company (founded 2017, backed by the St Baker Energy Innovation Fund) that owns and operates one of the country's largest DC fast and ultrafast public charging networks across 300+ locations in every state and territory. Evie is a charge point operator (CPO) and e-mobility service provider: it buys energy and resells it as charging sessions to drivers and fleets, and builds and operates charging assets for site hosts, councils, dealerships and commercial property owners.

## API posture

Evie Networks publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-07-27 there is no developer
portal, no API documentation, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDKs, no
sandbox, no `/.well-known/` discovery document and no published authentication or
onboarding path. A real production API gateway exists at `https://api.goevie.com.au`
(Google Cloud Endpoints / ESPv2) serving the Evie Charging mobile app, but it is
undocumented, unadvertised and answers anonymous callers with
`{"message":"The current request is not defined by this API.","code":404}`.
Charger location data reaches developers only indirectly, through third-party
aggregators (PlugShare, Open Charge Map, state EV-charger datasets) that Evie
does not operate.

Evie is **not** a designated data holder under Australia's Consumer Data Right
energy regime — the CDR Register's public energy data-holder brand summary lists
84 brands and Evie is not among them — so the statutory data mandate that
produced identical APIs across Australian banks and energy retailers does not
reach the EV charging layer.

## Notes for agents

- Do not treat `https://evie.com.au/wp-json/` as an Evie API. The marketing site is WordPress and its core REST API is open anonymously (813 routes across 31 namespaces, including `wpsl/v1` store-locator records for charging sites). It is CMS plumbing documented by WordPress, with no product intent, terms of use or stability commitment.
- Do not treat `https://api.goevie.com.au` as a public API. It is private mobile-app plumbing with no documentation, contract, terms or onboarding.
- There is no route by which a third party can obtain an individual Evie customer's charging-session data, and no open network, utilisation, availability, pricing or emissions feed.
